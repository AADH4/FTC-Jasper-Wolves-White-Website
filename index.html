About Wolves White

We are a dedicated team of students driven by curiosity, collaboration, and a passion for robotics. Since our founding in 2021, we've grown from an ambitious vision into a competitive force in FIRST Tech Challenge, combining engineering excellence with community leadership.

Our mission extends beyond competition—we're committed to inspiring the next generation of engineers and innovators through hands-on experience, rigorous design, and meaningful outreach.
