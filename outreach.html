Upcoming Initiatives & Events

School Tour

PISD Tour

We are taking our engineering journey on the road! The Plano ISD Tour involves visiting local schools to demo our robot, share our mechanical design processes, and ignite an interest in competitive robotics among younger students.

Workshops

Davis Library Workshops

Education should be accessible to everyone. We host free community workshops at Davis Library, providing local kids with hands-on STEM lessons, interactive building challenges, and foundational coding guidance.

Fundraiser

Team Fundraisers

Supporting our technical goals requires community backing. Come visit us at our upcoming bake sales, where we raise critical funds for raw materials while running live robot drive demonstrations for patrons.

Giving Back

STEM Kits for Hospitals

We believe in spreading hope through innovation. Our team designs, builds, and distributes custom STEM educational kits to local youth hospitals, offering children creative and engaging engineering projects during their stays.
